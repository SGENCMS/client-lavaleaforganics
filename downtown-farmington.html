Directions & Parking

Getting here from Downtown Farmington → Lava Leaf (Main St / US-64 Eastbound)

  1. From Downtown Farmington, get onto Main St (US-64) and head east.
  2. Stay on E Main St. It’s a simple, mostly straight drive along the main corridor.
  3. Continue until you reach 5100 E Main St, Suite 105 (Lava Leaf is in the plaza at that address).
  4. Pull into the main parking lot and look for Suite 105 signage.

Landmarks & Drive Time from Downtown

We're about 3.5 miles east of downtown Farmington — roughly 8 to 10 minutes on E Main St (US-64), depending on lights.

Heading east from the downtown Main St corridor, you'll pass:

  • Farmington Museum & Gateway Park as you leave downtown
  • San Juan College, north of Main on the way out
  • Animas Valley Mall and the retail stretch around it
  • Target, Starbucks, and Chick-fil-A — you're within a minute of us here
  • Olive Garden on your right, then the 5100 E Main St plaza

If you reach Outback Steakhouse or Chili's, you've gone slightly too far — turn around and look for the plaza with Suite 105 signage.

Free on-site parking, easy in and out. From the far west side of Farmington, add about five minutes. Coming from elsewhere in town? See all our Farmington delivery service areas.

Parking Notes: Free on-site lot at the plaza - typically easiest in/out before 9am, 11:30am–1pm, and after 6pm on weekdays.

Free parking is available directly in front of our downtown Farmington dispensary, with additional spots in the lot behind the building.


Fast Pickup, Friendly Team

Walk in, get checked in, and head to the counter - most visits are quick and straightforward. Our team can help you find popular categories fast and answer questions. We accept cash and debit (ATM available if needed). Store entry and parking are designed for easy access.
