First Visit? Here's How to Order

What to bring. You must be 21 or older with a valid, unexpired government-issued ID — a driver's license, state ID, passport, or military ID all work. Out-of-state IDs are fine, and you don't need to be a New Mexico resident. We check at the door every visit, and again at the counter. That's standard compliance, not a sign anything's wrong.

Recreational and medical. We serve both adult-use customers and registered medical patients. If you're shopping as a patient, bring your New Mexico medical cannabis card along with your ID for tax-exempt pricing.

Purchase limits. New Mexico caps adult-use purchases at 2 ounces of flower, 16 grams of concentrate, and 800 milligrams of edibles per transaction. Registered medical patients work from a separate 90-day allowance. Our system tracks this at checkout, so you'll know where you stand before you pay.

Finding us. We're at 5100 E Main St, Ste 105, in Go To Plaza on E. Main between Durango Joes and the Bow & Arrow Rambler Tap Room, near Taco Libre and Crumbl Cookie. Free parking sits directly in front, and the store is wheelchair accessible. We're an easy drive from anywhere in the Four Corners — roughly 20 minutes from Kirtland, 25 from Bloomfield, and 30 from Aztec.

Ordering ahead. Browse the menu, add what you want, and reserve online. You'll get a confirmation, and your order will be waiting at the counter — just bring your ID. Walk-ins are always welcome too.
